The first five minutes

Emergency rooms start with questions.
Panic erases the answers.

It is 2 a.m. Your father is on the floor, struggling to speak. At the hospital the triage nurse asks, fast:
What medicines does he take?Any drug allergy?Heart problem or stent?On blood thinners?When did he last eat?Who is his doctor?
You know the answers on a normal day. In that moment, most people go blank. This kit puts every answer on paper, in the order a doctor needs it, before the emergency happens.
Without it / With it

The difference is minutes of confusion.

Without the kit
  • Family guesses medicine names and doses
  • Allergy is remembered late, or not at all
  • Old reports are hunted for in the car
  • Every relative gives a different history
  • A slow decline goes unnoticed until it is serious
With the kit
  • One card in the wallet names allergies and who to call
  • One page goes straight into the triage nurse's hand
  • Medicines, devices and baseline are written and verified
  • Anyone in the family can step in and hand it over
  • A 30-day tracker shows the trend, not just today

FAQ

Good to know

Is this medical advice?

No. It organises information you supply; it does not diagnose or recommend treatment. In an emergency call 112 or your local emergency service first.

How long does it take?

About 5 minutes per person with prescriptions in hand. Unknown fields can stay blank.

Is my family's data safe?

The live product should clearly explain what is processed on your device, what is transmitted for payment, and whether anything is stored. Check the published privacy policy before entering sensitive information.

Will it fit on a card?

Yes. The card is wallet size. Long entries are shortened with a warning so you can keep only the essentials.

Who should use it?

Parents and grandparents, anyone with a condition, allergy, implant or long-term medicines, and travellers.

Can I make it for several people?

Yes. Choose the number of people at checkout and fill one form after another.
